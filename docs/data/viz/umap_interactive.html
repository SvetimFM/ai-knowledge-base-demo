What is UMAP?

UMAP (Uniform Manifold Approximation and Projection) reduces 768-dimensional document embeddings to 2D for visualization. Documents with similar content appear close together.

How to interpret: Clusters indicate thematically related documents. Distance reflects semantic similarity - nearby points discuss similar topics.
