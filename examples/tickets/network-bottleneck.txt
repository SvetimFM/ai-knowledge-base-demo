Title: Suspected network bottleneck in distributed training

We're experiencing what appears to be a network bottleneck during distributed training across our HPC cluster. The GPUs are underutilized (60-70% usage) during communication phases, and we see high latency spikes in our profiling tools.

Environment:
- 32 nodes with 8 H100 GPUs each
- InfiniBand HDR (200 Gbps)
- NCCL 2.19
- PyTorch 2.1 with FSDP

The issue is most noticeable during gradient synchronization in our transformer model training. CPU usage is normal, GPU compute is fast, but communication phases show significant delays.

We've already tried:
- Increasing NCCL buffer sizes
- Enabling NCCL tree algorithms
- Checking physical network connections

Looking for advice on diagnosing and resolving HPC network performance issues.
